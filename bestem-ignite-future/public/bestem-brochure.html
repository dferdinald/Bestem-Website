About BeSTEM

BeSTEM Innovation Hub is a leading STEM education organization in Ghana, dedicated to empowering young minds through hands-on learning experiences. We create innovation labs where learners build, experiment, and solve real-world challenges.


Our Mission: To transform STEM education by making it practical, engaging, and accessible to all students aged 5 and above.

Why Choose BeSTEM?

  • Hands-On Learning: Practical, project-based approach that brings theory to life
  • Real-World Skills: Preparing students for careers in technology and innovation
  • Expert Instructors: Passionate educators with industry experience
  • Modern Facilities: State-of-the-art labs with cutting-edge equipment
  • Small Class Sizes: 10-30 students for personalized attention
  • Certification: Industry-recognized certificates upon completion
  • Flexible Schedules: Weekend classes designed for students

Learning Journey

  1. Discover & Explore: Problem identification and brainstorming
  2. Design & Plan: Design thinking and prototype development
  3. Build & Create: Hands-on construction with cutting-edge tools
  4. Test & Iterate: Solution testing and continuous improvement
  5. Share & Impact: Community presentations and knowledge sharing
